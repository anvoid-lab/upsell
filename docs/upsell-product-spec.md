# VendAI — Your Automatic Sales Assistant for WhatsApp, Instagram & Facebook

> Stop losing customers who go silent. Let AI bring them back.

---

## The Problem

Every day, small businesses lose sales — not because their product is bad, but because the conversation was never followed up.

The customer asked. You answered. They said *"I'll get back to you"* — and disappeared.

You don't have time to follow up on every conversation. And when you do, it's already too late.

---

## What is VendAI

VendAI is a **unified inbox** that brings together all your customer messages from WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook into one single place — and automatically sends personalised follow-up messages when a customer goes silent without buying.

Not a generic template. A message that sounds like it was written by you.

> Starting with WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram. More channels coming soon.

---

## How It Works

**1. Connect your accounts**
Link your WhatsApp Business, Instagram, and Facebook to VendAI in under 5 minutes.

**2. One inbox for all your messages**
See every customer conversation from all three platforms in a single, clean interface. No more switching between apps.

**3. AI watches every conversation**
When a customer goes silent after asking about a product, VendAI detects it automatically.

**4. A personalised follow-up is sent**
A few hours later, the customer receives a message written by AI — with their name, the product they asked about, and a reason to come back.

**5. You close the sale**
When the customer replies, you take it from there. VendAI did the hard work of bringing them back.

## Built-in Sales Techniques

VendAI doesn't just send reminders — it applies proven sales techniques automatically:

- **Urgency** — alerts customers when stock is running low
- **Upsell** — suggests complementary products based on what the customer asked about
- **Social proof** — shares reviews from other customers about the same product
- **Cart recovery** — re-engages customers who showed interest but didn't buy

## What It's Not

VendAI is not a chatbot that replies instead of you. It doesn't replace your voice or your relationship with the customer. It's an assistant that handles the follow-up work so you can focus on closing.
