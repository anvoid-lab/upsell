# Keep the Seller in Control

## Product principle

The Sales Copilot supports the seller's judgment. It does not independently conduct the commercial conversation.

## Recommended output

For each situation, provide:

- a concise situation assessment;
- the most useful missing information;
- one recommended next action;
- the reason for that action;
- an optional draft reply;
- warnings or uncertainty;
- alternatives when context supports more than one reasonable path.

## Human decision points

The seller should decide whether to:

- send or modify a suggested message;
- disclose commercial terms;
- offer discounts or exceptions;
- make commitments or guarantees;
- continue follow-up;
- involve another stakeholder;
- disqualify the opportunity.

## Avoid

- Autonomous sending.
- Presenting uncertain interpretations as facts.
- Hiding missing context.
- Optimizing for conversion at the expense of consent, trust or policy.

## Success criterion

The seller can understand and evaluate the recommendation before acting.
