# First Response to an Inbound Instagram DM

## Objective

Recognize why the person contacted the business and make the next interaction easy without delivering an immediate generic pitch.

## Recommended structure

1. Acknowledge the specific trigger or request.
2. Provide the promised information when the trigger involved a promise.
3. Offer one relevant next step or ask one useful question.
4. Keep the first response concise.

## Adaptation by trigger

- Product question: answer the known factual part, then clarify fit if necessary.
- Story reply: refer to the topic or response that started the conversation.
- Keyword request: deliver the promised resource before adding qualification friction.
- General interest: ask what outcome or problem prompted the message.

## Avoid

- Treating every trigger as equal purchase intent.
- Requiring several answers before delivering something already promised.
- Long feature lists.
- Generic mass-message language.
- Continuing unwanted contact.

## Success criterion

The person receives what they expected and has one clear, low-friction way to continue.
