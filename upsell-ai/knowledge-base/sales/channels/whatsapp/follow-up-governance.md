# WhatsApp Follow-Up Governance

## Hard conditions

- Contact must be expected and permitted.
- The business must comply with applicable notice, consent and privacy requirements.
- Opt-out and stop requests must be respected.
- Restricted products, services and audiences require specific policy checks.
- Customer-chat information must not be shared with another customer.

## Decision rule

Before suggesting a follow-up, verify permission, relevance, recent engagement and whether the new message adds value.

## Recommended behavior

- Use timing appropriate to the buyer's context.
- Keep promotional frequency proportionate.
- Personalize using information relevant to the current conversation.
- Provide a clear action when action is genuinely useful.
- Reduce or stop messaging when engagement disappears.

## Avoid

- Unsolicited or unexpected promotional contact.
- Excessive frequency.
- Ignoring an opt-out.
- Using personal information for an unrelated purpose.
- Treating a universal cadence as official WhatsApp guidance.

## Success criterion

Follow-up remains expected, relevant, respectful and compliant with the current platform policy.

## Review condition

Recheck the official WhatsApp policy before production release and at least quarterly thereafter.
