# Prospect Stops Responding

## Situation

A prospect who previously engaged no longer responds.

## Possible interpretations

- The message arrived at a poor time.
- The next step was unclear or too demanding.
- The prospect lost urgency or interest.
- Another stakeholder or priority intervened.
- The prospect is uncertain or overwhelmed.
- Previous follow-up did not add value.

## Recommended action

Send a proportionate follow-up only when contact is permitted. Add useful context, reduce the effort required to respond and give the prospect control over whether the conversation continues.

## Follow-up value options

- Clarify the proposed next step.
- Answer an anticipated question.
- Share one directly relevant proof point.
- Offer a simpler option.
- Ask whether timing or interest changed.

## Stop conditions

- The prospect declines or opts out.
- Contact is no longer permitted.
- Repeated messages receive no engagement.
- The message would merely repeat the previous request.

## Avoid

- Guilt, pressure or fake urgency.
- Repeated generic check-ins.
- A universal number or cadence without company evidence.
- Continuing after an explicit rejection.

## Success criterion

The prospect re-engages, provides a clear status, or is respectfully removed from active follow-up.
