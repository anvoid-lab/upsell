# Contact Is Not the Decision-Maker

## Situation

The current contact needs approval from a partner, manager, owner, procurement function or another stakeholder.

## Recommended action

Understand the decision process without diminishing the current contact's role. Help them communicate the business case and, when appropriate, ask to include the relevant stakeholder.

## Information to discover

- Who participates in the decision?
- What does each stakeholder care about?
- What criteria will be used?
- What happens after internal agreement?
- What information would help the current contact?
- Is direct access appropriate and welcomed?

## Possible next steps

- Prepare a concise summary for internal sharing.
- Invite the stakeholder to a conversation.
- Answer the questions the contact expects to receive.
- Agree on when the internal discussion will happen.

## Avoid

- Calling the contact “not the real decision-maker.”
- Going around the contact without agreement.
- Assuming approval is a formality.
- Overloading the contact with generic material.

## Success criterion

The buying process and stakeholders become clearer, and the current contact is better equipped to advance the decision.
