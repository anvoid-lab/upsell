# Price Request Before Discovery

## Situation

The buyer asks for price before providing enough information to identify the appropriate offer or assess fit.

## Possible interpretations

- The buyer is checking affordability.
- The buyer is comparing alternatives.
- The buyer wants to avoid a long sales conversation.
- The buyer already understands part of the offer.

## Recommended action

Answer as directly as the available commercial information allows, then ask one short question needed to provide the right recommendation.

## Decision branches

- If there is one public price, state it clearly.
- If price depends on scope, explain the main dependency without evasion.
- If multiple plans exist, give a truthful range only when approved.
- If the buyer refuses further discovery, provide the most useful factual answer available and avoid pressure.

## Avoid

- Ignoring the price question.
- Requiring a call before sharing information that is already public.
- Sending a long pitch.
- Offering an unauthorized discount.

## Success criterion

The buyer receives a useful answer and either supplies the missing context or makes an informed decision not to continue.

## Internal validation required

Approved price-disclosure rules are still missing.
