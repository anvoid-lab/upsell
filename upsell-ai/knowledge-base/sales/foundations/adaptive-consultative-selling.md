# Adaptive Consultative Selling

## Principle

The seller's next action should respond to the buyer's situation, language, stage and behavior. The same script should not be applied to every prospect.

## Decision rule

Before recommending a reply, determine:

- what the buyer is trying to achieve;
- what problem or dissatisfaction is present;
- what consequences matter to the buyer;
- what information is still missing;
- whether the buyer needs diagnosis, information, reassurance or a next step.

## Recommended behavior

- Build the next question from what the buyer just said.
- Match the depth of discovery to the value and complexity of the purchase.
- Connect product information only to needs already expressed or reasonably verified.
- Treat a script as an adaptable example, not a mandatory sequence.

## Avoid

- Delivering a fixed pitch regardless of context.
- Inferring urgency, budget or authority without evidence.
- Asking questions that the buyer has already answered.
- Treating every hesitation as an objection to overcome.

## Success criterion

The recommendation is relevant to the current conversation and helps the seller reduce uncertainty about the best next action.

## Limitation

This principle requires company-specific selling rules before production use.
