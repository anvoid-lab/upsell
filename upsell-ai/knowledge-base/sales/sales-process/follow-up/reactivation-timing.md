# Follow-Up Timing and Reactivation Cadence

## Purpose

Define the temporal rules, frequency limits, and timing criteria for re-engaging stalled conversations across messaging channels.

## Principle

Follow-up timing must respect customer availability, communication norms, and platform policies. Re-engagement is only justified when introducing new context, assistance, or time-sensitive relevance.

## Decision rule

When scheduling or suggesting a follow-up:
- If a customer explicitly requested contact at a specific time, honor that time exactly (`send_in: timestamp`).
- For active conversations interrupted by brief silence, wait 24 to 48 hours during business hours before sending a gentle check-in.
- For stalled prospects with no reply after two attempts, transition to passive dormancy or require explicit human intervention.
- Never send promotional follow-ups outside local commercial business hours.

## Recommended behavior

- Reconnect by referencing the specific item or topic previously discussed.
- Provide a frictionless, low-pressure path to reply or ask questions.
- Acknowledge when a lead appears inactive without guilt-tripping language.

## Avoid

- Repeated rapid messaging within hours of an unread message.
- Messaging late at night or early in the morning in the tenant's timezone.
- Continuing automated nudges after an opt-out or explicit expression of disinterest.

## Success criterion

The follow-up generates either a resumed positive conversation or an explicit status confirmation from the customer while preserving brand goodwill.
