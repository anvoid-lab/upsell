# Upsell and Cross-Sell Governance

## Purpose

Govern when and how a seller may introduce complementary products (cross-sell) or upgraded options (upsell) without jeopardizing the core sale.

## Principle

Expansion offers must enhance the primary purchase value, not create distraction or friction during decision-making.

## Decision rule

Before suggesting an upsell or cross-sell, confirm:
- The customer has already shown high intent or confirmed satisfaction with the primary item.
- The complementary product is compatible and adds clear immediate value.
- The suggestion is introduced as optional and subordinate to the main transaction.

## Recommended behavior

- Suggest complementary accessories only after the core item is selected or agreed upon.
- Present upgrades by highlighting specific differentiated benefits rather than mere price differences.
- Keep the tone helpful: "Customers who choose X often pair it with Y because..."

## Avoid

- Pushing higher-tier items when the customer has expressed budget sensitivity.
- Introducing unrelated or distracting cross-sells before securing agreement on the primary choice.
- Presenting optional add-ons as mandatory prerequisites.

## Success criterion

The customer feels their total purchase experience is enhanced, either accepting the add-on willingly or declining without friction toward the main sale.
