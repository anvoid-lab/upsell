# Secure an Explicit Next Step

## Situation

The current conversation has produced enough value or clarity to justify a small commitment.

## Recommended action

Propose one concrete next step that is proportional to the buyer's readiness.

Examples of commitment types:

- answer one remaining qualification question;
- review a relevant option;
- include another stakeholder;
- select a meeting time;
- confirm a proposal requirement;
- complete a purchase step.

## Good next-step properties

- Specific.
- Easy to understand.
- Appropriate to the current stage.
- Mutually agreed rather than assumed.
- Includes timing when timing matters.

## Avoid

- Ending with a vague “let me know.”
- Asking for a meeting before establishing relevance.
- Presenting several competing calls to action.
- Treating politeness as commitment.

## Success criterion

The buyer accepts, declines or modifies a specific action, giving the seller clearer information about readiness.
