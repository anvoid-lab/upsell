# Minimum Viable Qualification

## Objective

Determine whether there is enough fit and readiness to justify the next commercial investment.

## Core dimensions

- Fit: the buyer's need can be served by the offer.
- Problem or goal: there is a meaningful outcome to pursue.
- Readiness: the buyer has a reason to act within a relevant period.
- Ability: the commercial conditions are potentially viable.
- Decision path: the person can decide or can involve the necessary stakeholder.
- Commitment: the buyer is willing to take a proportionate next step.

## Proportional use

For a simple or low-value purchase, confirm only the information needed to recommend the right option. For a complex B2B purchase, also investigate measurable value, stakeholders, decision criteria and decision process.

## Recommended behavior

- Qualify progressively across the conversation.
- Use existing context before asking new questions.
- Treat unknown information as unknown rather than negative.
- Disqualify respectfully when there is a clear mismatch.

## Avoid

- Applying an enterprise checklist to a simple purchase.
- Interpreting interest as purchase readiness.
- Assuming that the current contact is the sole decision-maker.
- Forcing budget disclosure without explaining its relevance.

## Success criterion

The seller knows whether to advance, continue discovery, nurture, refer or disqualify.

## Internal validation required

Company-specific ICP, affordability, urgency and disqualification thresholds are still missing.
