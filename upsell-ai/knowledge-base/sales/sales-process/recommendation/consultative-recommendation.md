# Consultative Recommendation

## Purpose

Guide the seller to propose products or solutions that directly match confirmed buyer requirements rather than generic catalog dumping.

## Principle

A commercial recommendation is effective only when the buyer can clearly see how the product solves their specific stated need, budget, or context.

## Decision rule

Before recommending a product or service, verify:
- At least one specific buyer need or preference has been expressed or confirmed.
- The item is in stock and matches the tenant's current commercial policies.
- The rationale explicitly links the product features to the buyer's criteria.

## Recommended behavior

- Limit options to one primary recommendation and at most two relevant alternatives.
- Explain *why* the recommended item fits the customer's stated context.
- Follow the recommendation with an explicit verification question to gauge reaction.

## Avoid

- Overwhelming the buyer with entire catalog categories.
- Recommending premium items before establishing interest or budget context.
- Claiming product features that are not verified in tenant knowledge.

## Success criterion

The customer acknowledges that the recommendation addresses their situation and agrees to evaluate the next step.
