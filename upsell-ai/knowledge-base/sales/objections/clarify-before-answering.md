# Clarify Before Answering an Objection

## Principle

An objection may describe a surface concern rather than the underlying barrier. Diagnose it before attempting to resolve it.

## Recommended sequence

1. Acknowledge the concern without becoming defensive.
2. Pause and avoid an immediate long explanation.
3. Ask one neutral clarifying question.
4. Confirm the concern in the buyer's own terms.
5. Respond only to the confirmed issue.
6. Check whether the response resolved that concern.

## Useful question patterns

- “What part of that concerns you most?”
- “When you say it is too expensive, what are you comparing it with?”
- “What would you need to feel comfortable moving forward?”
- “Is the main concern the timing, the investment, or the expected result?”

These are patterns, not scripts. The seller should adapt them to the conversation.

## Avoid

- Interrupting or contradicting the buyer.
- Assuming every objection must be overcome.
- Responding with a feature dump.
- Offering a discount before diagnosing the concern.
- Using unsupported proof or guarantees.

## Success criterion

The underlying concern becomes clearer, enabling a relevant response or an honest decision not to proceed.
